What you will do

Each trial plays two 3-second clips of the same moment of a collapse, from a player's own camera, one after the other: A, then B. One of them is the full simulation. The other is what that player's netcode drew from the data it received. Sometimes the two are identical. Nothing is marked: watch the whole scene as you would in a match.

  • Pick the clip that looked wrong. If you can't tell, guess. Guesses are part of the measurement: they are how "can't tell" is measured.
  • Then rate how much it would bother you in a match, from 0 (not at all) to 4 (ruins it).
  • Keys: A / B to pick, 0 to 4 to rate, R to replay both.
  • View the page large, ideally full screen. The page records the size you watched at.
