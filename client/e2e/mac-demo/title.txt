vibe-land on a Mac

PhysX 5 GPU rigid bodies + native destruction
running on Apple Silicon (M3 Max) through Metal, via CuMetal

Server: web-fps-server, VIBE_PHYSICS_BACKEND=physx_gpu
Client: Chromium, WebGL on ANGLE/Metal, WebTransport

Live capture of the real game client against the live server.
An automated player walks, drives and fires; the city demolition
is triggered through the server's debug endpoint. The physics
itself is not scripted or pre-rendered.
