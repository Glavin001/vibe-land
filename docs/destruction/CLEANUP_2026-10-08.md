## Invalid: remove or abandon

### A. The impact solver family

This covers PhysX `feat/impact-capacity` and its dev branches. The common
error: it computes impacts with its own dynamics and contact handling instead
of leaving them to PhysX, then feeds the result back into PhysX.

| Item | Where | What it does that is invalid |
|---|---|---|
| Impact capacity "E" (ADMM) | `PxgDestructionImpact.cuh`, `VIBE_IMPACT_CAPACITY` (already retired, off) | Its own contact cone program and impactor dynamics. |
| Implicit impact step "B" | `PxgDestructionImpactStep.cuh` | Its own impactor-plus-patch dynamics with contact rows. |
| Explicit impact step "D" | `PxgDestructionImpactExplicit.cuh`, `VIBE_IMPACT_STEP` / `PX_DESTRUCTION_IMPACT_EXPLICIT` (**still on in high.env**) | Its own explicit dynamics of the impactor and patch. Its verdict replaces the solver's, and with the bounds it controls the impactor through PhysX. |
| Contact routing | `PX_DESTRUCTION_IMPACT_ROUTE` (**still on in high.env**) | Moves contacts out of PhysX's rigid result into the step. Only meaningful with the step. |
| Per-impactor and pairwise contact bounds | `contactPairMaxImpulse` in the gpusolver contact preps | Impulse caps inside PhysX's contact solver. |
| Anchored-chunk contact bound | `PX_DESTRUCTION_ANCHORED_CONTACT_BOUND`, `dev/anchored-ghost-log` | An impulse cap; it also produced ghosts. |

## Open question for the owner: how impacts should load the stress solver

Under the architecture, an impact reaches the solver only as the contact
impulses PhysX reports. Two of today's findings still apply:
- the trial pass grades against a dead stop on anchored chunks (the 39 MN truck over-count);
- the infinite wall: PhysX stops the impactor before any joint breaks.

Within the rules, the candidates are in the stress solver's input model:
- how the reported impulse is converted to a load over the contact's duration (a dynamic load factor, with a cited model);
- whether the solver's verdict in the trial pass, followed by PhysX's native re-simulation with the broken hierarchy, already lets the impactor through once joints break.

That needs a measured baseline with all the impact machinery off before any new
design.
