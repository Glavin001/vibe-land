# Physical authoring assumptions

These are game-scale material models, not certified structural-engineering values. The native gravity and destruction measurements determine acceptance. Units are metres, kilograms, seconds and pascals.

Bulk timber, stone, brick, glass and metal begin with the existing authoring library's material table. Timber uses density 600 kg/m³ and modulus 10 GPa. Painted framing panels represent a timber/plaster composite, rather than a solid 180 mm thickness of hardwood: effective density 320 kg/m³, modulus 2 GPa, compression elastic/fatal 6/12 MPa, tension 0.6/1.2 MPa and shear 1/2 MPa.

Chunks within one solid timber piece retain bulk strength. Connections between separate timber pieces use a joint material: compression 8/16 MPa, tension 0.5/1 MPa, shear 1/2 MPa and modulus 1 GPa. Siding and dentils use a weaker fastener interface: tension 0.1/0.2 MPa and shear 0.25/0.5 MPa. The reported area is the measured physical touching face, never an enlarged synthetic patch.

Furniture joints represent the reduced area of dowels/glue within that face. Four 8 mm dowels at about 5 MPa have an approximately 1 kN tensile capacity, equivalent to 0.1 MPa across a 100 × 100 mm leg end. The furniture interface uses tension 0.05/0.1 MPa, shear 0.1/0.2 MPa and compression 3/6 MPa. The chair uses half these interface strengths to represent its smaller effective mortise/glue area, giving an 80 N tensile limit across a 40 × 40 mm leg end. Its solid timber and collider contact areas remain unchanged. Internal tabletop and leg fracture seams retain bulk timber strength. These joints do not attach loose tables, chairs, beds or sofas to the building.

Glazing is 6 mm thick, density 2500 kg/m³, with the existing brittle glass constitutive model. The glazing bedding interface has tension 0.2/0.4 MPa and shear 0.3/0.6 MPa. Panes rest on sills with a gap below their window heads, preventing them from becoming columns carrying the storeys above.

Fitted fixtures attach only across real faces touching the floor or wall. The standalone toilet has a hollow cistern and a hollow, broad pedestal. Beds use perimeter rails and slats; pillows use bedding density. Refrigerators use an effective insulated-panel material (280 kg/m³, 1 GPa modulus, 0.5/1 MPa elastic/fatal tension) representing metal skins and insulation within their authored panel thickness, rather than solid stone. Fences share coincident posts rather than placing duplicate colliders in one volume. Footings are the only zero-mass nodes; they extend below the asset's ground plane. No part of the acceptance workflow freezes debris, disables gravity, caps fracture work or suppresses destruction.

The native bridge delivers shots through moving rigid spheres. A small sphere at high speed can cross a thin pane in one step because native destruction does not support CCD in this SDK. Review shots therefore use explicit speeds, radii and close origins; straight ray hits are recorded before firing. Momentum alone does not determine damage to a loose table: an increasingly heavy but slow projectile mostly moves the table as a whole. The furniture case uses a faster downward impact so the supporting legs take a meaningful load.
