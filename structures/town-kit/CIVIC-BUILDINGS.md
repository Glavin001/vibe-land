# Civic additions: library, cinema and fire station

Three independent, furnished, destructible building candidates extend the town kit.
They are included in the separate 67-building civic-town preview. The original
64-building scene and the live `/city` deployment remain unchanged.

| Builder / asset | Footprint | Storeys | Chunks | Layout and identity |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `buildNeighborhoodLibrary` / `neighborhood-library` | 14 × 14 m, plus portico | 1 | 1,656 | Brick walls, tall recessed windows, four-column pedimented entrance, reading tables, two book-stack rooms, reception and rear exit |
| `buildArtDecoCinema` / `art-deco-cinema` | 12 × 18 m, plus marquee | 1 | 2,055 | Stepped Art Deco front, teal marquee, physical RIALTO lettering, framed geometric film posters, side pilasters, foyer, 36 upholstered seats, central and cross aisles, projection screen and three secondary exits |
| `buildFireStation` / `fire-station` | 14 × 14 m | 2 | 2,219 | Two open engine bays, equipment store, supported upper floor, dogleg stairs, mess/kitchen, two-bed dormitory and washroom |

All doors supplied as leaves are parked open. Other openings are actual empty
passages. Windows, trim, marquee, portico, roof, columns, furniture and fittings
are physical chunks; only buried foundations are fixed. Loose props rely on
contact. Construction has real bonded contacts and no hidden collision walls.

`buildBookStack()` supplies three loose hardbacks, nine chunks total: two covers
and one paper block per book. Books are not welded to one another or to shelves.
`buildCinemaSeat()` supplies a 20-chunk upholstered chair. Both are separate,
reusable exports; the library and cinema compose them through `placeProp`.

## Authoring and exports

Import builders from `src/index.mjs`. Each returns `{pack, metadata}`. Options
include `mirrored`, `furnished` and the existing six `palette` choices. Library
and fire-station masonry accepts `brickColor`; the cinema accepts `facadeColor`
and `accentColor`. Floor counts are deliberately fixed at the documented values.
Use `composeScene` for translation and quarter-turn placements.

```sh
node scripts/build-civic.mjs
node scripts/build-civic.mjs --mirror
node --test tests/civic-buildings.test.mjs
node scripts/check-civic-routes.mjs
```

Individual exports are standard ScenePack v2 inside `out/<asset>.json.gz`, with
separate `<asset>.meta.json` files. Mirrored names end in `-mirror`. Separate
`book-stack` and `cinema-seat` prop packs are generated by the first command.
The metadata retains room bounds, entrances, routes, cameras, material options,
geometry results, raw asset hashes and dependency provenance.

## Preview and evidence

The kit preview at `http://127.0.0.1:6174/?asset=neighborhood-library` accepts each
asset name. Use the saved room and exterior cameras, or move the camera freely.

```sh
TOWN_KIT_IMAGE_FORMAT=jpeg node scripts/screenshots.mjs neighborhood-library
TOWN_KIT_IMAGE_FORMAT=jpeg node scripts/screenshots.mjs art-deco-cinema
TOWN_KIT_IMAGE_FORMAT=jpeg node scripts/screenshots.mjs fire-station
```

Captures retain 1600 × 1000 resolution. JPEG quality 92 is optional to limit disk
use; PNG remains the default. Contact sheets are composed from decoded local
images to avoid browser thumbnail-loading failures. The browser uses available
shared memory for transient render buffers rather than redirecting them to the
nearly full shared disk. On this host, `TMPDIR=/dev/shm` also keeps the
disposable browser profile in RAM; Playwright cleans it up when the browser
closes. Saved assets and review outputs still live inside the kit.
`TOWN_KIT_RENDERER=software` is an explicit visual-only
fallback and is recorded in the report; native reviews always use physx-2 GPU.

Reports and contact sheets live under `out/reviews/<asset>-visual/`.
Failed/incomplete capture reports must not count as visual passes. Earlier
iterations remain in `out/reviews/history/`; only unusable images from incomplete
capture attempts were removed, with their failure reports and a cleanup manifest
retained. No physics recordings or unrelated files were removed.

## Acceptance status

- All three furnished buildings and their mirrors pass geometry validation:
  zero unintended overlaps, valid convex geometry and anchored construction.
- Four tests pass: deterministic output, mirrors, quarter-turn repeated instances,
  independent bond identities, room-route metadata coverage, empty variants and
  modest loose-prop fragmentation.
- Static route checks pass for base and mirror layouts. These sample a 0.7 m-wide
  clearance box from 0.3 m above the route to 2.1 m headroom. They deliberately
  skip stair-climbing segments and **do not replace native capsule traversal**.
- Native gravity stability and full capsule walking **pass for all three base
  buildings**, including 30 simulated seconds at rest with zero spontaneous
  breaks/crushing. The cinema and fire-station mirrors pass too. Capsule routes
  reach 18 library, 20 cinema and 84 fire-station checkpoints per orientation.
- Both standalone props pass native gravity stability. Seven valid intact pose
  audits pass; the mirrored library has no passing intact recording.
- Window damage and furniture damage **pass inside each base building**.
- Full demolition qualification remains **failed/incomplete**. The mirrored
  library is rejected at native frame 1 (error 64, 16 iterations, unconverged,
  unobserved). Removing books and then all furniture does not fix it; the empty
  mirrored shell has zero external contacts. Exact-reflection controls are
  retained under `diag-library-shell*` for independent investigation.
- The stronger masonry breach cases break structure but have not passed their
  settled, traversable-opening gate. Support-loss collapse is not qualified.
  Standalone book/seat impact tests also need further work, despite passing rest
  tests and the successful seat breakage inside the cinema.

The drive was initially too full for native review. After space became available,
those tests ran; disk space is no longer the reason these remaining cases fail.
`node scripts/civic-status.mjs` inventories current asset hashes and relevant
per-case metadata, retaining failures and missing reviews instead of granting a
release. `out/reviews/civic-status.json` is the machine-readable inventory.

Run GPU cases sequentially:

```sh
node scripts/review-civic.mjs stability traverse
node scripts/review-civic.mjs glazing wall furniture collapse
node scripts/audit-rest.mjs --civic
node scripts/civic-status.mjs
```

The runner covers base/mirror buildings and applicable standalone prop modes,
stops at the first failed case by default, and records exactly which modes were
requested. `TOWN_KIT_CONTINUE=1` measures all independent requested cases while
retaining a failed exit status. `TOWN_KIT_CIVIC_ASSETS=fire-station,cinema-seat`
selects a bounded subset; it never qualifies untested assets.
It retains the existing solver, gravity, convergence, rest and damage gates.
Inspect native recordings from several angles and audit resting poses before
marking these candidates ready to import. One-storey collapse tests measure roof/ceiling displacement and do not demand
nonexistent upstairs furniture. Multi-storey tests retain the furnishing-fall
requirement. Empty structural target sets fail. Neither test accepts unsettled
or unconverged damage. Full release qualification remains withheld.


The exact-reflection control now reproduces the native rejection with an empty
1,034-chunk shell: the original passes, the reflected copy fails, using identical
harness and SDK hashes and zero external contacts. See
[the bounded reproduction](repros/library-reflection/README.md). This narrows the
investigation but does not prove the root cause or excuse the failed variant.


The earlier 32-view intact review and selected damage replays were inspected directly.
`out/reviews/civic-gallery.jpg` retains that earlier exterior/interior overview;
`civic-visual-inspection.json` records the visual conclusions and remaining work.
Native replay recordings can be scrubbed from the preview at any camera angle.


Recorded walkthrough: `out/reviews/fire-station-traverse/traverse.webm`.
Recorded local destruction: `out/reviews/art-deco-cinema-furniture/furniture.webm`.

## Cinema detail revision

The current cinema adds 121 physical chunks for two framed film posters, the
RIALTO name board and lettering, and eight side pilasters. Poster artwork is
coarse geometry attached through backing panels; there are no floating decals,
fixed decorative pieces, or new route obstructions. The new helper is
`src/parts/cinema-details.mjs`.

Both orientations passed eight native cases in total: stability, traversal,
glazing, and furniture damage. Each case first completed 30 simulated seconds of
observed, converged intact rest with zero spontaneous damage. Each traversal
visited 20 checkpoints. The resting-pose audit passed for both cinema versions.
Thirteen intact views, including the poster and seat closeups, were visually inspected;
rendering reported zero errors. The refreshed 67-building preview contains this
revision. Full demolition qualification remains withheld; see the current
`out/reviews/civic-status.json` rather than treating older reports as current.

A separate mortar-joint experiment is documented in
`repros/civic-masonry/README.md`. Its failed candidates have not been promoted
into the cinema or the town.


Current detailed-cinema demolition rechecks remain failures: both masonry breach
cases settle but block the gameplay capsule; the base support-loss case hits
PhysX fetchResults/CUDA error 700; the mirrored support-loss case breaks 1,218
bonds but does not achieve the required roof collapse or settled rest. These
failures are preserved in the current reports and do not count as passes.
